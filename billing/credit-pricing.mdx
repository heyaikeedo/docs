Aikeedo prices every model from what the provider charges you. You set two numbers, **credits per USD** and a **markup**, and Aikeedo works out how many credits each token, image, second or character costs your users. You can give a model its own markup, or charge fixed credits that don't follow the cost at all.

<Info>
  Credit pricing replaces the credit ratios of Aikeedo 5.2 and the Aikeedo Price Calculator spreadsheet. On 5.2 or older? See [Commercial Resources](/overview/commercial-resources#on-v5-2-or-older).
</Info>

## How a price is worked out

For each unit a model bills, such as an input token, an image or a second of video:

```text
credits per unit = provider cost (USD) × markup × credits per USD
```

- **Provider cost** comes from the model catalog that ships with Aikeedo, or from a cost you enter yourself.
- **Markup** is the model's own markup if it has one, otherwise your default markup.
- **Credits per USD** is how many credits a US dollar of provider cost is worth.

With 100 credits per USD and a 1.25× markup, a request that costs you $0.01 costs your users 1.25 credits.

A rate set to **fixed credits** ignores the cost: users pay those credits whatever the provider charges. A model's [discount](/advanced/models#discounts) is taken off after the price is worked out.

## Open the Credit pricing page

Go to **Settings → Finance → Credit pricing**, or open **Credit pricing** from the **More** menu on the [Models](/advanced/models) page.

<Frame><img src="https://cdn.aikeedo.com/docs/v5.3/credit-pricing.png" alt="Credit pricing page with filters and model list" /></Frame>

The page lists every model with its rates:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Model** | The model, with marks such as **Own markup** when it has a markup of its own. |
| **Your cost** | What the provider charges you per unit, in US dollars. |
| **Users pay** | The credits users pay per unit. |

Under the filters, **Provider prices as of** shows the date we last checked the catalog's provider prices.

Filter by **Provider**, or by **Status**:

- **New provider price**: the catalog lists a price that differs from the one in use. See [Price updates](#price-updates).
- **Fixed credits**: at least one rate charges fixed credits.
- **No price**: users pay nothing for some of the model's usage.

Each row's menu has **Details**, **Open model** (the model's [Pricing tab](/advanced/models#pricing-tab)) and **Provider prices**, which links to the provider's own price list.

<Warning>
  A model with **No price** is free for your users for the usage that has no price. Set its price, or switch the model off.
</Warning>

## Pricing settings

Click **Pricing settings** to set the two numbers every cost-based price uses.

<Frame><img src="https://cdn.aikeedo.com/docs/v5.3/credit-pricing-settings.png" alt="Pricing settings modal with credits per USD and default markup" /></Frame>

<ParamField path="Credits per USD" type="number" required>
  How many credits a US dollar of provider cost is worth. With 100, a request that costs you $0.01 costs users 1 credit, before your markup.

  Providers bill in US dollars, so this is always in dollars, whatever currency you sell plans in.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Default markup" type="number" default="1.25× (20% margin)">
  What you add to the provider's cost. Models without a markup of their own use it. Enter it as a **% margin** or as **× your cost**.
</ParamField>

### Margin or multiple

Choose how you enter a markup with **Markup as**:

- **% margin**: the share of what users pay that you keep. A 20% margin means \$0.20 of every dollar users spend is yours and \$0.80 pays the provider. A margin must be under 100%.
- **× your cost**: a multiple of the provider's cost. 1.25× and a 20% margin are the same markup.

For more than 3× your cost, use a multiple.

### Review the change

Saving opens **Review changes**. It shows how many settings change, how many models change price, and that new prices apply from the next request. Models with fixed credits keep them.

Tick **I understand users keep the credits they have, but each one buys less from now on** (or "more", when the change goes the other way), then click **Confirm and save**.

<Note>
  Changing credits per USD changes what every existing credit is worth. Users keep their balance, but each credit buys more or less than before.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Upgraded from 5.2?** Credits per USD is not set after the update. Until you set it, cost-based rates price nothing and only fixed credits charge users. The **Pricing settings** button shows a red dot while it's unset, and the dashboard shows **Credit pricing not set**.
</Warning>

## Per-model markup

To charge more or less for one model, open the model's **Pricing** tab and click **Customize** next to **Markup**. Enter the markup as a margin or a multiple. **Use default** puts the model back on your default markup.

The tab shows the default markup and what users pay with the model's markup. On the Credit pricing page, the model is marked **Own markup**.

## Rates

Each rate shows how it is **Priced by**:

- **Cost-based**: credits follow your cost, markup and credits per USD. You can keep the catalog's cost or enter your own.
- **Fixed credits**: users pay these credits, whatever your cost.
- **Billed in dollars**: for [WaveSpeed](/integrations/wavespeed) models. The hub bills each run in US dollars, and users pay that, plus your markup, in credits.

A model can bill several rates, such as input and output tokens, or a price per video for each resolution and length. A request is priced at the rate whose conditions it meets, the most specific first.

### Fixed credits

Use fixed credits when you want a price that doesn't move with the provider's cost, such as a round number of credits per image.

Fixed credits never follow cost changes: [Price updates](#price-updates) and changes to credits per USD leave them as they are.

<Info>
  After updating from 5.2, every credit ratio you had set is kept as fixed credits on the matching rate, so your installation charges exactly what it did before. Switch a rate to **Cost-based** when you're ready.
</Info>

## Price updates

Aikeedo updates bring new provider prices in the model catalog. They never change what users pay on their own.

When the catalog lists a cost that differs from the one in use, the **Price updates** button shows a count. Click it to review the changes:

- Cost-based rates show what users pay now and after the update, for example **Users pay 1.5 → 1.25 credits**.
- Fixed rates show **Fixed credits: users pay the same**.

Click **Apply new prices** to use them, or **Not now** to keep the current prices. A cost you entered yourself is never replaced.

## Hub prices

Models from hubs are priced in their own way:

- **fal.ai**: priced per fal.ai unit, such as an image, a megapixel, a second or a video. The price is read from fal.ai's pricing API when you look the model up or add it, and needs your fal.ai key. It isn't refreshed afterwards. See [fal.ai](/integrations/falai).
- **WaveSpeed**: each run is quoted by WaveSpeed's price API before it starts, and users pay that quote × markup × credits per USD. The estimate shown before a run uses the model's base price. See [WaveSpeed](/integrations/wavespeed).

## Plan credit limits

When you edit a plan, the credit limit field shows the most credits its price covers at your markup, with **Use it** to fill it in. If the limit is above that, a warning says users who spend all their credits cost you more than you charge.

- **Yearly plans**: credits renew every 30 days, so each renewal gets a twelfth of the yearly price.
- **Lifetime plans**: the price can't cover renewals for long, so keep the limit low.
- **Plans in other currencies**: add an exchange rate to US dollars under **Settings → Finance → Exchange** to see the hint. See [Exchange Rates](/billing/exchange-rates).

The hint needs credits per USD to be set.

## What users see

Users see what each model costs on the **Pricing** page of the app, at `/app/billing/pricing`. It's in the workspace settings menu, and the Plans page links to it with **See what each model costs**.

<Frame><img src="https://cdn.aikeedo.com/docs/v5.3/user-pricing-page.png" alt="User Pricing page with type tabs and model prices" /></Frame>

- Tabs by type: **Chat**, **Image**, **Video**, **Voiceover**, **Transcription**, **Voice isolation**, **Embeddings** and **Tools**.
- Each model shows what it's billed for, in credits. In [session-based usage mode](/billing/overview#session-based-usage), prices show in your site currency.
- Models outside the user's plan are marked **Not in your plan**. Prices that depend on the request are marked **Varies by request**.
- Discounted models show the full price struck through.

Until models have prices, the page shows **No prices yet**.
