## Introduction

Aikeedo can issue an invoice for each paid order through an **invoicing engine**: a plugin that connects to an invoicing or accounting service. The service numbers the invoice, records the payment and produces the PDF. Aikeedo keeps track of each invoice, retries when the service is unavailable, and gives the PDF to the customer and to you.

Aikeedo doesn't number invoices or produce invoice PDFs itself. Without an engine, orders are not invoiced, and customers still get a [purchase confirmation](/email/emails#purchase-confirmation) that works as a receipt.

<Note>
  Invoicing engines are available as plugins. Aikeedo ships with **No invoicing** selected. Developers can build an engine for any service. See [Invoicing Engine](/development/plugins/guides/invoicing-engine).
</Note>

## Choosing an engine

1. Install the invoicing engine plugin. Installed engines are listed under **Settings → Finance → Invoicing engines**.
2. Go to **Settings → Billing** and, under **Invoicing**, choose the **Invoicing engine**.
3. Click **Configure selected engine** and enter the service's details. This page belongs to the plugin.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Which payment methods are invoiced is set in the engine's own settings. Free orders are never invoiced.

## When an order is invoiced

When a paid order is fulfilled, Aikeedo asks the engine for an invoice in the background, so the payment never waits for the invoicing service. The invoice is:

- **Pending** while Aikeedo is waiting for the service.
- **Issued** once the service has created it. The invoice number comes from the service.
- **Failed** when the service refused it, or after the last retry.

The PDF is fetched from the service each time someone downloads it. Aikeedo doesn't store a copy.

### Retries

If the service can't be reached, or returns an error, Aikeedo tries again later, waiting longer each time: after 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 12 hours and then once a day. It gives up after 8 attempts, or straight away when the service says the invoice can't be issued as it is, for example because of invalid billing details.

Retries are run by the **Retry invoices** system task, so cron must be running. See [Background Jobs & Queue](/advanced/queue).

## On the order page

Open an order under **Orders** in the admin panel. The **Invoice** section shows:

- The status: **Issued**, **Pending** or **Failed**, with when the next attempt is due.
- **Engine**: the invoicing engine used.
- **Invoice number**.
- **Attempts** and the **Last error**, if any.

You can:

- **Retry now**: try a pending or failed invoice again straight away. Fix the cause first, for example the engine's settings or the customer's billing details.
- **Create invoice**: invoice a paid order that wasn't invoiced, for example one whose payment method the engine skips, or one placed before you set up invoicing.
- **Download PDF**: download the issued invoice.

The section is hidden when invoicing is off and the order has no invoice.

## What customers see

Customers find their orders in the app under **Billing**. On an invoiced order, the **Invoice** section shows **Being prepared** until the invoice is issued, then the invoice number and **Download invoice**.

The [purchase confirmation email](/email/emails#purchase-confirmation) waits for the invoice and arrives with the PDF attached. If the invoice isn't ready within an hour, the email is sent without it, and an **Invoice ready** email follows with the PDF once it is issued.

## Billing details on invoices

Invoices use the billing details the customer enters at checkout, including the company name and tax ID of business customers. Turn on **Billing address** under **Settings → Billing** to collect them. See [Billing address](/billing/overview#billing-address).
