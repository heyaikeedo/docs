We're looking for well-built plugins and themes from the community to list alongside the official add-ons on [aikeedo.com](https://aikeedo.com/marketplace/), so the people running Aikeedo can find them.

## How a community listing works

  <div className="flex gap-2 items-start"><span className="flex h-[1lh] shrink-0 items-center"><Icon icon="square-rounded-check" size="18" /></span><span>**You support it.** Questions, updates and compatibility with new Aikeedo releases are yours to handle.</span></div>

## What to send

Email [sales@aikeedo.com](mailto:sales@aikeedo.com) with:

- **What it is** — the name, what it does, and who it's for, in a few sentences
- **Where it lives** — the page where people can get it, plus documentation if you have it
- **What it looks like** — screenshots or a short video, or a link to a live demo
- **Compatibility** — the Aikeedo versions you've tested it against
- **Support** — where customers reach you, and who maintains it

## Before you get in touch

Install it on a clean Aikeedo installation and go through it once: install, configure, use, update, uninstall. The packaging pages cover what a solid release looks like.

We look at every add-on ourselves and reply either way. If we list it, we'll confirm the details with you first.
