[fal.ai](https://fal.ai/) is a hub of image and video models from many developers, such as Veo, Seedance, Seedream and FLUX. Aikeedo ships with a set of fal.ai models, and you can add any other fal.ai image or video model by its endpoint id.

## Connect fal.ai

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    Sign in to [fal.ai](https://fal.ai/), open **API Keys** in your dashboard and click **Add Key**. Copy the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the key in Aikeedo">
    Go to **Settings → FalAI**, paste the key into **API Secret** and click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the models on">
    Go to **Settings → Models**, filter by the **FalAI** provider, check the prices and switch on the models you want to offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep your API key secret. It gives access to your fal.ai account and its balance.
</Note>

## Models

The fal.ai models in the catalog include Veo 3.1, Veo 3.1 Fast and Veo 3.1 Lite, Gemini Omni Flash 1.2, Seedance 2.5 Flash, Seedream 5.5 and FLUX.2 [flash].

Updates add fal.ai's default models switched off. The other catalog models are under **More → Import models** on the Models page. See [Import models](/advanced/models#import-models).

### Add any fal.ai model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new model">
    Go to **Settings → Models** and click **Add model**. Choose **FalAI** as the provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look the model up">
    Type the endpoint id, such as `fal-ai/flux-2/flash`, or paste the model's page link from fal.ai. Click **Look up at the hub**.

    Aikeedo reads the model's details and parameters from fal.ai, and its price from fal.ai's pricing API. Change anything you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the model and switch it on">
    Check the price on the **Pricing** tab, then save and switch the model on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The price lookup needs your fal.ai key. Without one, the model is added without a price, and users would use it for free until you set one.
</Note>

You can also add a fal.ai model by hand. See [Add a hub model](/advanced/models#add-a-hub-model).

## Pricing

fal.ai bills each model in its own unit: an image, a megapixel, a second or a video. Aikeedo charges in the same unit:

```text
credits = units fal.ai reports × cost per unit × markup × credits per USD
```

The cost per unit is read from fal.ai when you look the model up or add it, and isn't refreshed on its own. When fal.ai changes a price, enter the new cost on the model's **Pricing** tab. For the catalog's fal.ai models, new prices arrive with Aikeedo updates under [Price updates](/billing/credit-pricing#price-updates).

See [Credit Pricing](/billing/credit-pricing) for markup and credits per USD.

## Safety settings

fal.ai image models follow **Settings → Features → Privacy & safety**. When safety is off, Aikeedo asks fal.ai for its most permissive moderation for each model that supports it.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The lookup finds no price">
    Save your fal.ai key under **Settings → FalAI** first, then look the model up again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Generations fail">
    Check that the key is correct and that your fal.ai account has a balance. Some models need access to be granted in your fal.ai account first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
